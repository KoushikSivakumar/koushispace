NEXUS started as a portfolio project and slowly became something I actually want to keep using.

The idea is straightforward: connect GitHub history, infrastructure signals and engineering context, then turn that history into useful intelligence instead of another dashboard full of noise.

## the constraint

I don't want NEXUS to look like a generic AI SaaS template. The interface is deliberately dense, monochrome and evidence-oriented. The product should feel like a tool first and a portfolio piece second.

## the stack

The current foundation is built around FastAPI, PostgreSQL, SQLAlchemy, Alembic and Docker. The model layer is designed around repositories, commits, pull requests, reviews, issues, releases, workflow runs and deployments.

```text
GitHub
  ↓
ingestion
  ↓
PostgreSQL
  ↓
analysis / ML
  ↓
engineering intelligence
```

## what comes next

The next step is making the data useful: incremental ingestion, repository-level analytics, anomaly signals and an AI layer that can explain what changed and why it matters.

That is the interesting part. A polished dashboard is nice. A system that can look at months of engineering history and tell you something you hadn't noticed is much more useful.

> build the infrastructure first. make the interface earn its space.
