This is where I want to keep the smaller lessons that don't deserve a full tutorial.

Things like why a database model became complicated, why a UI looked wrong even though every component was technically correct, and what happened when an apparently simple feature touched five other parts of a system.

The point is not to manufacture wisdom. It is to keep a record of the work.

## the format

Short posts. Real screenshots when useful. Code when it explains something better than prose. No ceremony.
